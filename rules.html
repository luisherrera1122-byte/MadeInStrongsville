Scoring Format

Half-PPR (0.5 points per reception). On top of standard scoring, any completed pass, rush, or reception of 40+ yards earns a +0.5 point bonus — and if that same 40+ yard play ends in a touchdown, it earns an additional +0.5 point on top of that.
